What is happening at Brew Haven

10 August 2026

New autumn menu launch

We are excited to introduce our new autumn menu featuring pumpkin spice lattes, apple cinnamon muffins, and hearty butternut soup. Available from 15 August until 30 September.

05 August 2026

Latte art workshop

Join us on 20 August for a hands-on latte art workshop led by our head barista. Learn to pour hearts, rosettas, and tulips. R150 per person including materials and a pastry.

28 July 2026

Best New Coffee Shop award

Brew Haven has been awarded "Best New Coffee Shop" at the 2026 Johannesburg Community Business Awards. Thank you to all our loyal customers!

15 July 2026

Extended hours for exams

We are now open from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM during exam season. Free Wi-Fi and unlimited filter coffee refills for R35.

01 July 2026

Partnership with local farmers

We are proud to partner with Mzansi Coffee Growers. 30% of our house blend is now sourced directly from local small-scale farmers.

20 June 2026

Charity coffee morning success

Our Charity Coffee Morning raised R5,200 for the Johannesburg Children's Shelter. The next one will be on 19 July for a local animal rescue.
